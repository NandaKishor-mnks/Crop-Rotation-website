CROP ROTATION

Awareness on Crop Rotation among Farmers — Gangavaram Village

Introduction


Crop rotation involves growing different types of crops sequentially on the same piece of land across successive farming seasons[cite: 1]. Practicing crop rotation improves soil health, optimizes nutrients, and breaks recurring pest and disease cycles without heavy reliance on synthetic fertilizers[cite: 1].

How It Works


Paddy → Black Gram / Green Gram → Next Crop


Alternating heavy nutrient-consuming crops like Paddy with leguminous pulse crops (e.g., Black gram or Green gram) fixes free atmospheric nitrogen directly into the earth through plant root nodules[cite: 1].
